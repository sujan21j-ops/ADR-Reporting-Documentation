2. Chronological Case Narrative
A fictional 35-year-old female was prescribed oral amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily for a dental infection, with treatment assumed to have started on 20 October 2026. On 21 October 2026, approximately two hours after the third dose, she reportedly developed generalized urticaria and lip swelling. The scenario states that the suspected medicine was discontinued and urgent medical assessment and treatment were required. Symptoms improved after medicine discontinuation and treatment, but complete resolution and the exact treatment provided are not documented. No information is available about previous allergy history, concomitant medicines, relevant investigations, or alternative causes. Rechallenge was not performed. Based on the limited simulated information, the relationship is assessed as possible for training purposes only. Seriousness and expectedness must be verified against the actual clinical details and applicable reporting requirements. This is a fictional learning exercise, not a real patient report.
